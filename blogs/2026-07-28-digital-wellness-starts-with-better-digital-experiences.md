Every August, National Wellness Month invites us to slow down, reconsider our habits, and make more space for physical, mental, and emotional well-being. It is usually associated with personal choices: taking breaks, sleeping better, managing stress, exercising, or becoming more mindful. But there is another side of wellness that deserves attention.
